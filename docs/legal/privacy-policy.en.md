## 2. The basic principle: your data stays on your phone
LifePilot works first of all **on your device**. Agenda, tasks, notes, finances, bank statement, health, food, travel and the other information you enter are stored on your phone. We do not upload them to our servers by default. If you turn on synchronization or connect to a server on your phone, the app explains it and you can turn it off.

### Your data stays yours
The data you enter in the app is **yours**: it does not become ours and we do not sell it. We process it only to give you the service you ask for, and at any time you can see it, correct it, take it with you or delete it (including the whole account).
**Critical data.** Health and wellbeing, finances and bank documents, identity and ID documents, precise location and private messages are always critical; you can mark other data as critical too. For critical data: we use it **only** for the single request you make; we do **not** use it to train artificial-intelligence models, for statistics, to profile you or for advertising; we do **not** share it with third parties other than the supplier who, on our behalf, is needed to carry out that request; it leaves the phone **only** with your explicit consent, specific to each category, which you can withdraw at any time and which is never required to use the other features.
**All other data** is used to make the app work; usage statistics, if you turn them on, are anonymous.
If any data leaves the phone we tell you **before**, saying where it goes and who processes it.

## 4. Why we process it and on which legal basis
- **Creating and managing your account**: identifying you by email and a confirmation code, keeping one account per person, restoring your data on another phone, checking the minimum age of 16 (performance of the service, Art. 6.1.b GDPR; legal obligation for age).
- **Keeping proof of your consents**: which document you accepted, in which version and when (duty to demonstrate consent, Art. 7.1 GDPR; legitimate interest).
- **Making the app work on your phone** (performance of the service you requested, Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
- **Sending your data to Theia with the external AI**: your **explicit consent**, specific for each category (Art. 6.1.a and, for health and other special-category data, Art. 9.2.a GDPR; consent under the nFADP). You give or refuse it for each category in the **Privacy Center** and can withdraw it at any time.
- **Notifications, reminders and briefings** you turn on (performance of the service / consent).
- **Security, abuse prevention and moderation** in LifeNetwork (legitimate interest, Art. 6.1.f GDPR; legal obligations).
- **Legal obligations** (e.g. authority requests, tax obligations for any payments).

## 5. Artificial intelligence (Theia)
Theia reads everything in the app on your phone to help you. When it answers with an external AI service (**open** AI models run on **Cloudflare (Workers AI)** infrastructure, through **our server**), your question and the parts of your data that are needed must leave the phone. Before doing so we ask for your consent, **per category**, with three choices: *ask every time*, *always allow*, *never*. If you do not consent, Theia answers without looking at that data. Photos and files (bills, receipts, PDF statements) leave only with your consent for that sending. When Theia does not know something it can search the web (DuckDuckGo, or Wikipedia as a fallback) through our server: only a generic search phrase derived from your question is sent, without your personal data; you can turn it off in Theia's Intelligence settings. **Body photos** (Body check) stay on your phone, leave it only with your consent given every single time (never "always"), are used only for that analysis and are neither stored nor used to train models.
- The "What was sent" log only keeps type, date and size, never the content.
- **AI can make mistakes.** Always check figures and information. Guidance on health and finances is general and does not replace a doctor, dietitian or advisor.
- We do not use your data to train models. [To be confirmed with the AI provider contract.]

- **Signals on the phone.** The game may notice, only on the phone and without sending anything, changes in your data (for example less sleep, less activity, very harsh phrases written to Theia) to gently suggest an activity. They are hints, not diagnoses; we do not analyse anyone's face, voice or posture. You can turn it off at any time from Theia's page.
