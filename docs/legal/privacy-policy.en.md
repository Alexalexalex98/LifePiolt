This notice explains how we process personal data when you use the LifePilot app ("**the app**"), in line with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP).

## 2. The basic principle: your data stays on your phone
LifePilot works first of all **on your device**. Agenda, tasks, notes, finances, bank statement, health, food, travel and the other information you enter are stored on your phone. We do not upload them to our servers by default. If you turn on synchronization or connect to a server on your phone, the app explains it and you can turn it off.

## 3. Which data we process
| Category | Examples | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Profile data | name, email, photo, bio, business card | phone (and, if you use LifeNetwork, visible to others according to your settings) |
| Agenda and productivity | tasks, events, notes, goals, files, shifts | phone |
| **Finances** (sensitive) | bank statement, transactions, budget, bills, taxes, investments, LifePoints | phone |
| **Health and wellbeing** (special category, Art. 9 GDPR) | sleep, steps, mood, workouts, weight, meals, fridge, Apple Health data if you connect it | phone |
| Travel and location | cities, hotels, itineraries; location only if you turn it on | phone |
| LifeNetwork and messages | posts, comments, messages, bookings, seminars, applications | phone and, when needed to deliver them, server |
| Technical data | app version, device type, errors (anonymous, only if error reporting is configured) | error-reporting server |
| AI requests | your questions to Theia and, **only with your consent**, the parts of your data needed to answer | sent to the AI service for the single request |

We do not collect data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not do commercial profiling.

## 4. Why we process it and on which legal basis
- **Making the app work on your phone** (performance of the service you requested, Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
- **Sending your data to Theia with the external AI**: your **explicit consent**, specific for each category (Art. 6.1.a and, for health and other special-category data, Art. 9.2.a GDPR; consent under the nFADP). You give or refuse it for each category in the **Privacy Center** and can withdraw it at any time.
- **Notifications, reminders and briefings** you turn on (performance of the service / consent).
- **Security, abuse prevention and moderation** in LifeNetwork (legitimate interest, Art. 6.1.f GDPR; legal obligations).
- **Legal obligations** (e.g. authority requests, tax obligations for any payments).

## 5. Artificial intelligence (Theia)
Theia reads everything in the app on your phone to help you. When it answers with an external AI service (**Google Gemini**, through **our server**), your question and the parts of your data that are needed must leave the phone. Before doing so we ask for your consent, **per category**, with three choices: *ask every time*, *always allow*, *never*. If you do not consent, Theia answers without looking at that data. Photos and files (bills, receipts, PDF statements) leave only with your consent for that sending.
- The "What was sent" log only keeps type, date and size, never the content.
- **AI can make mistakes.** Always check figures and information. Guidance on health and finances is general and does not replace a doctor, dietitian or advisor.
- We do not use your data to train models. [To be confirmed with the AI provider contract.]

- **Signals on the phone.** The game may notice, only on the phone and without sending anything, changes in your data (for example less sleep, less activity, very harsh phrases written to Theia) to gently suggest an activity. They are hints, not diagnoses; we do not analyse anyone's face, voice or posture. You can turn it off at any time from Theia's page.

## 9. Your rights
You have the right to: **access**, **rectification**, **erasure**, **restriction**, **objection**, **portability** and to **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing already carried out. How to exercise them:
- In the app, **Privacy Center**: export or delete data per category, change or withdraw consents.
- By writing to [privacy email]: we reply within one month.
You have the right to **lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC/EDÖB), in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, in other EU countries the authority of your country of residence.
We do not take decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on you.

## 10. Security
We use appropriate technical and organizational measures: on-phone storage with optional encryption and app lock, encrypted backup, encrypted communications (HTTPS), minimization of the data sent. No system is 100% secure: we recommend protecting your phone with a passcode or biometrics. In case of a data breach that presents a risk to you, we notify the authority and you within the legal deadlines.

## 11. Minors
The app is intended for people aged **at least 16**. We do not knowingly collect data from younger people; if we become aware of it, we delete it.

## 12. Cookies and similar technologies
The mobile app does not use advertising cookies. If we offer a website or a web version, its cookie policy is separate.
